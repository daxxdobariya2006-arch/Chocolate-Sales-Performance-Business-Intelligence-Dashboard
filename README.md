#  ChocoLens – Chocolate Sales Business Analytics

##  Project Overview
ChocoLens is an end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes chocolate sales data
using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI to identify sales trends, top products,
best-performing countries, sales channels, and salesperson performance.

##  Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-selling products
- Analyze sales by country and channel
- Study monthly sales trends
- Compare salesperson performance
- Understand the relationship between marketing spend, discounts, and sales
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard

##  Tools & Technologies
- Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SQL Server, Power BI, DAX

##  Key Business Insights

- Australia is the highest-sales country.
- Brazil is the second-highest-sales country.
- Retail is the dominant sales channel.
- 70% Dark Bar is the top-selling product.
- Arjun Mehta is the top-performing salesperson.
- December has the highest monthly sales.
- June has the lowest monthly sales.
- Boxes Shipped has a strong positive relationship with Sales.
- Marketing Spend has a moderate relationship with Sales.
- Discount has very little relationship with Sales.
